A birthday letter

My Mahak,

Happy Birthday. ❤️

There is one date I will never forget — 28 January 2026. It was the first day I saw you, the day I met you, and somehow the same day our engagement began.

Life gave me a beautiful surprise that day: you.

Thank you for being part of my life and for becoming such an important part of my future.

Today is your birthday, but honestly, I feel like I am the lucky one.

Forever yours,
Uday ♥

Happy Birthday, Mahak ♥
